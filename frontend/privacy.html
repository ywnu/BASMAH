Privacy Policy

Draft for internal review. Not approved for publication.

The BASMAH frontend is currently a development prototype. It displays demonstration records in the browser and does not send those records to a BASMAH backend. Changes made in the prototype are not stored permanently.

Before public deployment, this policy must be revised to accurately identify the responsible organization, the personal data collected, purposes and legal bases, hosting and processors, retention periods, users' rights, contact details, and any applicable Saudi Personal Data Protection Law requirements.

Do not submit real personal or sensitive information into the prototype.
